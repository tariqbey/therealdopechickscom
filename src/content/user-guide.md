# Dope Chicks Platform Guide

Everything you can do on Dope Chicks, for fans, creators and admins. This guide lives in the app (Settings → Help & Guide, and Admin → User Guide) and is also available as a PDF.

---

## 1. What Dope Chicks is

Dope Chicks is a premium creator platform. Creators publish photos, videos and immersive VR180 / 360 experiences, sell subscriptions, take paid video calls, receive tips and use the built-in AI Studio. Fans pay with **BREAD**, the platform's credit currency, and creators cash their earnings out as money.

**Three kinds of accounts**

- **Member (fan):** browses, subscribes, unlocks content, calls creators, tips, and messages.
- **Creator:** everything a member can do, plus publishing, selling and payouts. Creator accounts are reviewed by an admin before they can sell.
- **Admin:** runs the platform from the back office at `/admin`.

## 2. Getting started

### Sign up
1. Open the site and press **Sign Up Free**.
2. Enter your email, a password, a display name and your date of birth. You must be 18 or older.
3. Choose **Fan** or **Creator**. Creators go into a review queue and can browse right away, but can't sell until approved.
4. You start with a **welcome bonus of 100 BREAD**.

### Your profile
Settings → **Profile** lets you change your display name, avatar, cover image, bio and account type. Creators have a much richer editor on their own profile page (see section 5).

### Navigation
- **Explore / Creators:** the home page with featured creators, trending creators and categories.
- **AI Studio:** AI image, character and video generation.
- **Get BREAD:** buy credits.
- The icons on the right of the top bar are **Messages**, **Settings** and **Sign out**. Your BREAD balance is always shown next to your name.

---

## 3. BREAD, the platform currency

- 1 BREAD is worth about **$0.10**. The exact rate is set by the admin under Settings → Currencies.
- Buy BREAD from **Get BREAD** or from the **Buy BREAD** button that appears whenever you don't have enough for something. Packages run from 50 BREAD ($4.99) to 2,500 BREAD ($175). Payment is by card through Stripe Checkout.
- BREAD pays for everything: post unlocks, VR videos, video calls, tips and AI generations.
- **Creators keep 80%** of every sale. The exact share is set by the admin under Settings → Payout systems.
- See every purchase and spend under Settings → **Transaction History**.

## 4. For fans

### Finding creators
The home page shows **Featured Creators** (picked by the admin), **Trending Now** and a row of **categories**. Click any card to open a creator's profile. The green **Online now** dot means the creator has been active in the last five minutes.

### Subscribing
Creators offer subscription tiers such as Bronze, Silver and Gold, each with its own monthly price and perks. Press **Subscribe** on a tier. Subscriptions are billed through Stripe and can be managed with the **Manage Subscription** button on the creator's profile.

### Unlocking content
Locked posts show a blurred preview with the tier or price needed. Subscribe to the right tier or pay the unlock price in BREAD.

### VR videos
Creators' profiles have a **VR Experiences** row. Free videos play instantly; paid ones show a BREAD price. Press the card to unlock and watch.

**Using the VR player**
- **On a phone or computer:** drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom, click the picture to play or pause, double-click for fullscreen.
- **Controls bar:** play, skip 10 seconds back or forward, volume, the time slider (shows what's buffered and the time under your cursor), reset view, settings (quality, speed, projection), fullscreen.
- **Keyboard:** space or K to play, J and L or the arrow keys to skip, M to mute, F for fullscreen, R to reset the view.
- **Phone motion look:** tap the compass button to steer the view by moving your phone.
- **Cardboard mode:** tap the Cardboard button on a phone, turn it sideways and drop it into any Cardboard-style viewer for split-screen 3D.
- **VR headsets (Quest, Pico, and others):** open the video in the headset's browser and press **Enter VR**. A floating control panel appears: restart, skip, play or pause, recenter, mute and exit. Point and press the trigger to use it. Hold the trigger on the panel's top bar to move it. Grip hides or shows it. A or X instantly recenters the video. The panel hides itself while you watch; the trigger brings it back. Headsets without controllers use gaze: look at a button and hold your gaze to press it, look down to open the panel.
- **End screen:** when a video finishes you can replay it or jump to the creator's next video, which auto-plays after a short countdown.

### Video calls
Creators who take calls show a **Video Call** button with their price.

1. Press **Video Call**. If the creator charges per minute, choose how long you want with the slider. The sheet shows the length, the price and what you'll be charged.
2. Press **Pay & request call**. The BREAD is held in escrow, not yet paid to the creator.
3. The creator gets a ringing alert. You can wait on the call page or leave; you'll be notified when they accept. Requests expire after 24 hours and are refunded automatically.
4. When accepted, press **Join call**. Allow camera and microphone access.
5. During the call: mute, camera on or off, flip camera on phones, and the red button to hang up. A timer shows time used and time left. The call ends automatically when time is up.

**Refunds are automatic** when the creator declines, you cancel, the request expires, or the call never connects. On per-minute calls, unused minutes are refunded the moment either side hangs up.

### Tipping
Press **Tip** on a creator's profile, pick an amount (or type one), add a note if you like, and send. Tips are paid in BREAD.

### Messaging
Press **Message** on any creator's profile or open **Messages** from the top bar.
- Type to send text. Creators may reply with an AI assistant when they're busy; those replies are marked with a sparkle.
- Press the **camera** button to record a video message up to 60 seconds, review it and send.
- The **Video call** button in the chat header books a call without leaving the conversation. Call receipts appear in the thread.

### Notifications
Settings → **Notifications** lets you turn on push notifications so you're alerted for accepted calls, replies and new content, even when the site is closed. The site can also be installed as an app from the **Install** banner.

## 5. For creators

### Getting approved
After signing up as a creator, upload a photo of your government ID under Settings → Profile. An admin reviews it. Until you're approved, your profile isn't listed and you can't sell. You'll see your status on your profile.

### Editing your profile
Open your profile (**My Profile**) and press **✏️ Edit**. Four tabs:
- **Profile:** avatar, cover, display name, bio (with an AI bio writer), location, profession, interests, likes and your **category**, which decides where fans find you on the home page.
- **Details & Stats:** height, build, complexion, eye and hair color, ethnicity, measurements, zodiac sign.
- **Subscription Tiers:** create Bronze, Silver, Gold or custom tiers with a monthly price (minimum $4.99) and a list of perks. Tiers can be turned off without deleting them.
- **Calls & Video:** see Video calls below.

Press **Save Everything** when done.

### Posting content
On your profile open **Manage**:
- Upload photos or videos (or pick from your **Library** of AI generations), add a title and description, and choose **Public** or **Locked**. Locked posts need a subscription of the tier you pick.
- **Pin** a post to keep it at the top of your grid.
- If the admin has **post verification** on, new posts show *Awaiting review* until approved. Rejected posts show the admin's note. Fans never see pending or rejected posts.
- Tap any post to edit or delete it.

### VR videos
Also under **Manage**, the **Upload Immersive Video** panel:
1. Choose the file (MP4, up to 3.5 GB). The platform reads its size and suggests a format.
2. Confirm the **format**: VR180 or 360°, 2D or 3D (side-by-side or top-bottom), or a regular video for the virtual cinema.
3. Set a **price in BREAD** (0 = free), optional thumbnail, title and description.
4. Press **Publish**. The upload goes straight to storage in resumable chunks, so a dropped connection resumes where it left off.
5. The video is watchable from the original file immediately. In the background it's converted to adaptive streaming (4K, 1440p and 1080p). Your list shows *Processing HD* with a percentage, then *Ready*.

Change the price, hide or delete videos from the same list. You earn 80% of each unlock.

### Video calls
Settings → **Video Calls** (or Edit profile → Calls & Video):
- Flip **Paid video calls** on.
- Choose how you charge. **Flat price per call:** one price and a fixed length. **Pay per minute:** a per-minute price plus the shortest and longest call you'll take; fans pick the length and pay up front, and you're paid only for minutes used.
- **Video messages from fans** can be turned off if you don't want to receive them.

When a fan books you, a ringing banner appears on every page with **Accept** and **Decline**, and you get a push notification. Accept and press **Join call**. You're paid when the call ends.

### Tips and earnings
Tips arrive instantly. Everything you earn (unlocks, calls, tips) goes to your **earnings balance**, separate from the BREAD you spend as a fan.

### Payouts
Settings → **Payouts** shows your earnings balance in BREAD and dollars.
1. Enter the amount (there's a minimum, 100 BREAD by default), choose a method (PayPal, bank transfer, crypto and others the admin enables) and fill in the details.
2. Press **Request payout**. The credits leave your balance immediately and the request goes to the admin.
3. Track it in **History**: *pending* → *approved* → *paid*. A rejected request returns the credits with the admin's note. You can cancel a pending request yourself.

### AI Studio
Generate images, build a consistent AI character, or turn an image into a short video. Each generation shows its BREAD cost before you run it. Results land in your **Library**, from which you can post directly to your profile. **Generation History** keeps everything you've made.

### Messaging as a creator
You can always send video messages. The AI auto-reply answers fans in your voice when you're away; it's built from your profile details.

## 6. For admins

Open **Settings → Admin Panel** or go to `/admin`. The sidebar:

### Dashboard
Total earnings and month-over-month growth, BREAD in circulation and creator earnings owed, pending payouts and posts awaiting review, user counts by role, new users, a period earnings chart with a selectable range, the content sales mix, top 10 selling creators, top 10 spending fans and storage usage.

### Users
Search by name, email or ID; filter by members, creators, pending approval, moderators or admins; sort by registration, last login, name or balance. Click a row for the full record: email and verification, user ID, registration and last-login dates, last online, BREAD and earnings balances. From there you can view their profile, **log in as the user**, send a password reset, toggle creator status, set approval, grant moderator or admin, or delete the account. **Add user** creates an account directly. **Export CSV** downloads the list.

### Transactions
Every BREAD purchase, spend, refund, bonus and every creator earning and payout in one ledger. Search, filter by type, pick a date range, export CSV.

### Payouts
Creators' payout requests. **Approve** when you've checked the details, **Mark paid** after you've sent the money, **Reject** with a note (credits go back to the creator). Filter by status.

### Content Wall
Turn **post verification** on so every new post waits for your approval. Approve, reject with a note, pin or delete posts, and review approved and rejected ones.

### Featured
Choose and order the creators shown in the home page spotlight. Leave it empty to show the newest approved creators automatically.

### VR Videos
Every VR video on the platform with its creator, format, size, price, unlock count and status. Change prices, hide, watch or delete.

### Pages
The site's text pages: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Content Guidelines, DMCA, About, Contact and any you add. Written in Markdown with a preview. Each page can appear in the footer under Platform, Company or Legal. Pages render at `/p/slug`.

### Announcements
Banners shown at the top of every page. Choose the audience (everyone, creators or members), a style, an optional link, and start and end times. Toggle them on or off.

### Features
Every platform feature in one place, grouped as Admin panel, Platform, Engagement and Analytics. Built-in features have a switch that turns them on or off across the site. Items marked *Roadmap* aren't built yet.

### AI Monetization and Analytics
API cost versus BREAD charged for AI generations, per-type breakdowns, sign-ups and revenue over time.

### Security
Creator ID verification queue with the uploaded documents, approve or reject.

### Settings
- **General:** site name, support email, company name and address, meta description, logo, favicon, watermark, Google Analytics ID.
- **Pricing:** the price lists creators and fans choose from: BREAD deposit packages (each tied to a Stripe price), post prices, message prices, subscription prices, attachment prices, video call prices, per-minute rates, VR video prices.
- **Payment systems:** Stripe status and notes on adult-content processors.
- **Payout systems:** creator share percentage, referral commission, payout commission, request fee, minimum request and the payout methods creators may pick.
- **Currencies:** currency code and symbol, the credit name, and the money value of one credit.
- **Domain name:** steps to attach your own domain and where to record it.
- **Mailing, SMTP:** your outgoing mail server details.
- **Categories:** add, rename, hide or delete the categories creators pick from.
- **Design:** primary and secondary brand colors (applied live), menu style and sidebar side.
- **Display & preview:** demo content switch and *view as fan* / *view as creator*.

## 7. Money flow in one picture

1. A fan buys BREAD with a card (Stripe).
2. The fan spends BREAD on a creator (unlock, call, tip).
3. The creator's earnings balance receives the creator share (80% by default). The platform keeps the rest.
4. The creator requests a payout. Credits leave their balance into a pending request.
5. The admin approves, pays the creator by the chosen method, and marks it paid.

## 8. Troubleshooting

- **"Insufficient BREAD":** buy a package; the Buy BREAD button appears right where you need it.
- **Video call won't connect:** both sides must allow camera and microphone. Try **Retry connection** on the call page. If one side is on a very restrictive office or mobile network, switch to another network.
- **VR video is dark or low quality:** open settings (gear icon) in the player and pick the top quality. HD processing finishes a few minutes after upload.
- **Headset shows "Enter VR" greyed out:** use the headset's own browser; desktop browsers can't enter VR.
- **I posted but fans can't see it:** post verification is on; the post is awaiting admin approval.
- **Creator can't be called:** they haven't turned calls on, or your BREAD balance is too low.
- **Didn't get a notification:** turn on push notifications in Settings → Notifications and allow them in your browser.
- **Forgot password:** use *Forgot password* on the login page, or ask an admin to send a reset.

## 9. Glossary

- **BREAD:** the credit fans spend. Bought with money, worth about $0.10 each.
- **Earnings balance:** a creator's share of sales, in credits, until paid out.
- **Escrow:** BREAD held for a call before it's paid to the creator or refunded.
- **Tier:** a subscription level with its own price and perks.
- **VR180 / 360:** immersive video formats; 3D versions are side-by-side or top-bottom.
- **Content Wall:** the admin's post approval queue.
- **Payout:** converting a creator's earnings into real money.
